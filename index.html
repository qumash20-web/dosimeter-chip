IR Dosimeter Chip

Concept model of the detector built in this project. X-rays hit a pressed organic pellet, the pellet emits infrared light, a silicon PIN photodiode senses it, and a readout circuit outputs a frequency. Drag to rotate.

  1. X-ray photons

    The ionizing radiation to be measured enters from the top.

  2. Light-tight cover and window

    Keeps ambient light out and lets the X-rays reach the pellet.

  3. IR-emitting organic pellet

    Pressed organic converter powder. Under X-rays it emits infrared light (radioluminescence).

  4. Si PIN photodiode

    Turns the infrared light into a photocurrent. Response 400 to 1100 nm, peak sensitivity at 940 nm.

  5. Readout circuit and pins

    Translates the detector signal into a frequency output (Hz) that the pins carry out.

Concept illustration, not to scale. Layer order and materials follow the experimental setup described in the dissertation.
